Apple Pie

Back to Home

Description

Apple pie is a classic dessert made with tender apples, warm spices, and a flaky pastry crust. It is a comforting treat that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients

  1. Apples: Use fresh, firm apples such as Granny Smith or Honeycrisp, peeled, cored, and sliced.
  2. Filling: Apples tossed with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and a little lemon juice.
  3. Pastry: Two pie crusts, one for the base and one for the top.
  4. Egg wash (optional): Brush the top crust with beaten egg for a golden finish.

How to Make It

Apple pie takes a few simple steps, and the result is a warm, fragrant dessert with a crisp crust and tender apple filling.

  1. Prepare the Filling: Mix the sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice.
  2. Fill the Crust: Place one pie crust in a pie dish and add the apple mixture.
  3. Add the Top: Cover with the second crust, seal the edges, and cut a few vents in the top.
  4. Bake: Brush with egg wash if desired and bake until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
